Indonesian Manpower Law — Key Provisions

Standard Working Hours

5-day week: 8 hrs/day, 40/wk
6-day week: 7 hrs/day, 40/wk
Rest after 4 continuous hrs: ≥30 min
Note: hours = paid hours, not gross shift time

Overtime Limits

Max OT per day: 4 hrs
Max OT per week: 18 hrs
12-hr paid shift = 4 hrs OT on weekday
Food obligation during OT: ≥1,400 kcal

Weekday OT Rates (PP 35/2021)

Hourly rate basis: Monthly ÷ 173
1st OT hour: 1.5×
2nd OT hour+: 2.0×
11-hr paid shift (weekday): ~14.5 hr-equiv

Rest Day / PH Rates (5-day wk)

Hours 1–8: 2.0×
Hour 9: 3.0×
Hours 10–11+: 4.0×
11-hr rest day shift: ~27 hr-equiv

BPJS Employer Contributions

Kesehatan: 4.0%
JKK (mining, Kel. IV): 1.27%
JHT (old age): 3.7%
JP (pension): 2.0%
JKM (death): 0.3%
Total employer: ~11.27%
JKK varies 0.24–1.74% by risk group

Leave & Entitlements

Annual leave: 12 days (after 1 yr)
Long service: Per PKB/co. policy
THR: 1× monthly (after 1 yr)
Sick leave: Paid (sliding scale)

Recovery Time (ILO Guidelines)

Min. between shifts: 11 hrs recommended
<10 hrs recovery: HIGH fatigue risk
10–11 hrs recovery: Marginal
Includes commute in "obligation" time

Continuous Ops Note

Mining/processing may be exempt from standard OT caps
Requires ministerial permit (certain sectors)
Shift arrangements via PKB/collective agreement
UU 13/2003 Art.77(3), PP 35/2021
